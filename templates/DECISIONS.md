# Decisions

Status: normative  
Scope: project

Record only decisions that materially constrain future architectural,
methodological, or contractual work. This is a lightweight log, not a mandate
to create a formal ADR for every choice.

## Decision: Short title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** accepted | superseded
- **Context:** Why the decision was needed.
- **Decision:** What was decided.
- **Rationale:** Why this choice was made.
- **Consequences:** Important trade-offs or follow-up constraints.
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** Optional link or heading.
